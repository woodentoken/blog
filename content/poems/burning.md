cool blood\
knowing only\
that it keeps cooling\

planets spin until they cant\
and everything in their orbit\
falls into red-gray cakes of mountains\
carving perfect slices

smooth plains of vapor\
toxic and yellow\
fill the worlds\
we've stumbled across

the perfect confluence\
of subatomics and superatomics\
allowing us all\
to pay our taxes online
