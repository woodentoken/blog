I drove west to Utah\
there was fog and a farm\
and a dirt road cutting through it all\

white barn and white house\
green grass red dirt\

no visibility at all\

on a winding road\
through august trees\
people on bikes climbed past\

I had gone too soon\
and drove back down the hill towards Ohio\
