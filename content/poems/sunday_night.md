now and again\
you drop into my dreams\
and I get to feel\
paralyzed\
quietly\
in the dark

this time you were two\
one disapproving\
and the other tender\
but postponing politely\
like I had unknowingly overstayed a welcome

it is unwitting

now I am weighing the prospects of contacting you\
whether you have moved on better than I have\
how inconvenient can I be\
or - is it even fair to drop into another reality\
if only to say\
	like a dumb thump barreling through the morning\
I still exist

it is 4 am and I am still sick\
I am not tired anymore

it is 4 pm\
I am once again tired
