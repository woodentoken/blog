date = 2020-05-10

Thinking

about 27 years and 27 days\
of having a mother like you\
who inspires little minds\
with lessons found\
under rocks in an Autumn creek\
or in calm words on a colorful page

of the natural ways\
you build passion\
out of natural things\
and believe that less flowers are more flowers

of how proud it makes us\
to see you cry\
about riding a horse or\
fighting someone else's battle\
alongside them

of how the things you plant\
every Spring\
in the moldy boxes on the back deck\
are the most comforting things\
i have ever seen

about the joy you get\
from giving love and\
themed gifts\
and how willing to give those you are

of lessons on working hard\
and creating, having patience\
offering forgiveness\
and being there for someone

and thinking of how strong you seem\
when you might not feel it\
and how proud that makes me\
to be your son.
