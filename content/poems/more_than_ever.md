title = 'more than ever'
date = 2020-03-28
+++

Writing to feel better\
and to believe writing happens

for better or worse\
is everything shackled\
to therapy

pushing out words from a basement freezer\
plugged in\
along with the spiders\
on the very floor

is it all for show\
placing myself under labels of hurt\
and sad\
and 'not feeling so good'

no meaning at all\
none found in the words\
spoken in etched lines\
like they’ve been heard before\
and will be heard again

grooved wax hearts\
spinning in the basement

etched\
from routines and habits\
that get caught in the webs\
all the time
