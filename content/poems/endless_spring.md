title = 'endless spring'
date = 2023-03-10
+++

endless spring\
make the weary unburdened\
make flowers expand forever\
sunlight bake lightly\
puddles pool larger\
flooding\
make lawns erupt\
and swallow their homes

endless spring\
sprout trees under floorboards\
sprout a garden\
in the halls of summer\
engorge our senses with perfume\
such that sight fades from view\

endless spring\
bring these things\
unasked for
and make us see new colors
