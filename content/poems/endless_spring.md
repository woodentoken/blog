+++
title = 'endless spring'
date = 2023-03-10
+++

endless spring\
make the day unburdened\
make flowers expand forever\
sunlight bake lightly\
puddles pool larger\
flooding frogpoles\
make lawns erupt\
and swallow their homes

endless spring\
sprout trees under floorboards\
sprout a garden\
in the halls of the earth\
engorge our senses with perfume\
so all sights fade from view

endless spring\
bring these things\
unasked\
and make us see new colors.
