I am an Aerospace engineer focused on the modeling and control of bird-inspired morphing aircraft. For more details, see [Research](https://woodentoken.github.io/blog/research/).

I am currently a 4th year PhD candidate in the department of [Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://mae.ucdavis.edu/) at University of California, Davis.

I am the first PhD student in the Bio-Informed Research and Design Lab ([BIRD Lab](https://uc-bird-lab.github.io/bird-lab-website/)) under the supervision of Professor Christina Harvey.

My research is funded by the Army Research Laboratory.
